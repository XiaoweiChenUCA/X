I am an Assistant Professor of Engineering Physics at the University of Central Arkansas [(UCA)](https://uca.edu/physics/). I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Oklahoma State University in 2024, where I worked with [Prof. Guoliang Fan](https://ceat.okstate.edu/ece/faculty/guoliang-fan.html).

My research focuses on computer vision, robotics, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence, with applications in healthcare and assistive technologies. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent robotic and embedded systems that integrate perception, autonomous navigation, and human-centered design to support independent living and improve quality of life.

In addition to research, I teach undergraduate engineering courses and mentor students in hands-on research projects involving computer vision, robotics, embedded systems, and intelligent assistive technologies.

Research Interests
======
My research lies at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, embedded computing, and intelligent assistive systems.

From a fundamental research perspective, I am interested in developing methods for visual perception, indoor localization, camera pose estimation, object understanding, and autonomous navigation in complex indoor environments.

From an application perspective, my research focuses on translating these technologies into intelligent systems that can assist people in daily activities, particularly older adults and individuals with cognitive or physical limitations.

Current research interests include:

Computer Vision: camera pose estimation, indoor localization, object detection, image classification, scene understanding
Robotics: autonomous navigation, robotic perception, human-robot interaction
Assistive Technology: intelligent systems for healthcare, dementia care, and independent living
Embedded and Edge AI: embedded intelligent systems, sensor integration, real-time perception
Machine Learning: deep learning, transfer learning, multimodal intelligent systems
Human-Centered Intelligent Systems: integrating perception, autonomy, and user needs into practical engineering solutions

Teaching and Student Mentoring
======
My teaching focuses on hands-on and laboratory-based engineering education. I teach courses including Microelectronics, Microcontrollers, Computer Vision, and Signals and Systems, and I mentor undergraduate students in research involving robotics, computer vision, embedded systems, and assistive technologies.

I am particularly interested in creating research and project experiences that help undergraduate students develop technical independence, systems thinking, and practical engineering skills.
